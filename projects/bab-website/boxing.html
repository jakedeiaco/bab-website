Common Questions

Do I need experience to start boxing?

None at all. Boxing Fundamentals is built for complete beginners. You'll learn stance, footwork, and basic combinations on day one — with a coach guiding you the whole time. Most people are surprised at how quickly they pick it up.

Is boxing a good workout even if I don't want to compete?

Absolutely. Most of our boxing members train purely for fitness, stress relief, and the challenge of learning a real skill. Boxing is one of the best full-body workouts there is — and way more fun than a treadmill.

When can I start sparring?

Sparring is invitation-only from coaches — typically after you've built a solid foundation in fundamentals. We prioritize safety above all else. You'll know when you're ready because we'll tell you.

How far is Bay Area Boxing from San Mateo / San Carlos / Redwood City?

We're at 210 El Camino Real in Belmont — about 5 minutes from San Mateo and San Carlos, 10 minutes from Redwood City and Foster City. Easy on/off El Camino with parking on site.

Is my first class really free?

Yes. No credit card, no commitment. Just show up, train, and see if it's a fit. Sign up at bayareaboxing.net/free-class or call us at (650) 520-8681.
