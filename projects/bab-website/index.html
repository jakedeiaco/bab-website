Meet the Team

15+ years of real coaches, real results. The people behind Bay Area Boxing.

Bay Area Boxing - Belmont, CA
Head Coaches
JD
Jake Deiaco
Owner & Founder

Jake is the founding owner and general manager of Bay Area Boxing. He managed the Belmont location for the first 5 years (2010–2015), then ran presale and grand opening for the East Bay location (Bay MMA & Fitness). Jake holds a Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu under Brian Fernandez (Ralph Gracie Black Belt lineage) and has 11+ years of experience in the grappling arts and fitness management. He currently teaches CrossTrain classes and is at the front desk weekdays — here to help with all your training and fitness goals.

BF
Brian Fernandez
Head Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Coach

Brian was born and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area and has been training martial arts for 20+ years. In 1994, he received a Black Belt in Judo under Willy Cahill. After years of training and competition, he added a Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu under Ralph Gracie in 2007. Now retired from competition, Brian brings that elite-level experience to the mat as Head Instructor at Bay Area Boxing. His classes are 90 minutes — BJJ warm-ups, technique, and full-contact sparring. As a Black Belt, Brian can rank students up to his level.

PM
Phillip Mendoza
Head Muay Thai Coach

Phillip started martial arts training at a young age, picked up boxing in his teens, and began formally training and competing in Muay Thai in 2010. Over the years he developed a passion for teaching and a sharp eye for technique, with a focus on fundamentals — believing that building proper fundamentals is the most important part of improving in any discipline. He uses that knowledge to help people get in shape, learn self-defense, and genuinely enjoy the process.

AB
Alexey Bulankov
Head Boxing Coach

Alexey is a USA Boxing-certified coach and competitive boxer. His classes focus on overall athleticism with an emphasis on technique — specifically the strategic and tactical aspects of boxing: footwork, ring generalship, and fight IQ. His background in Soviet-style boxing training methodology gives students an edge in becoming intelligent, well-rounded, and well-conditioned boxers.

VC
Victor "Vick" Chen
Head Muay Thai Coach

Victor's passion for martial arts started at age 8 — Karate, powerlifting, and bodybuilding — all while developing as a fitness trainer and exploring the functionality of strength in combat. His martial arts training began in 1993, and he went on to coach and train alongside competitors who fought in Strikeforce and the UFC. From 2009, Victor trained at the world-renowned Sasiprapa Gym in Thailand for 6+ years, and he continues to train with top-level gyms in NYC, Amsterdam, and the UK. Coach Vick brings all of that depth to his Muay Thai fundamentals and pad work classes here at Bay Area Boxing.
