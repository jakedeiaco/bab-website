What Is Muay Thai?

Muay Thai is Thailand's national combat sport — known as the "Art of Eight Limbs" for its use of fists, elbows, knees, and kicks. It's one of the most effective striking arts in the world, and one of the best full-body workouts you'll ever do.

Common Questions

Do I need experience to start Muay Thai?

None at all. BayFit Kickboxing is built for all levels — you'll be thrown right in with coaching support every step of the way. Most people are surprised at how quickly they pick it up.

What's the difference between BayFit Kickboxing and Muay Thai Instructional?

BayFit is our high-intensity class format — Muay Thai-based striking and combos at fitness pace. Instructional goes slower and deeper: technique, pad rounds, and drilling with a partner. Most members start in BayFit and layer in Instructional as they progress.

Can I compete in Muay Thai?

Yes. We have an active competition team. Several of our coaches compete actively. If that's your goal, we'll build a path to get you there — on your timeline. Call us at (650) 520-8681 to talk about fight camp.

How far is Bay Area Boxing from San Mateo / San Carlos / Redwood City?

We're at 210 El Camino Real in Belmont — about 5 minutes from San Mateo and San Carlos, 10 minutes from Redwood City and Foster City. Easy on/off El Camino with parking on site.

Is my first class really free?

Yes. No credit card, no commitment. Just show up, train, and see if it's a fit. Sign up at bayareaboxing.net/free-class or call us at (650) 520-8681.
