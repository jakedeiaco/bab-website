Our Story

Bay Area Boxing opened its doors in Belmont in 2010 with a simple idea: give the Peninsula a real combat sports gym. Not a franchise. A place where people could actually learn to fight, get in the best shape of their lives, and be part of a community that pushed each other.

Fifteen years later, that's still exactly what we are.

We've grown into a 5,000 square foot facility on El Camino Real in Belmont — with a full weight room, six barbell racks, heavy bags, open mat space, and a coaching staff that competes at every level. We've watched members walk in for the first time not knowing how to throw a punch, and walk out months later with real skills, real confidence, and a gym family they didn't expect to find.

Boxing. Muay Thai Kickboxing. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Strength Training. Programs for every level, taught by coaches who actually train.

That's Bay Area Boxing. That's what 15 years looks like when you never cut corners.

What Makes Us Different

We're not a franchise.

No corporate playbook. No rotating cast of trainers. One gym, one team, built from the ground up over 15 years. When you join, you become part of something that has real roots.

Our coaches compete.

USA Boxing-certified coaches. Active Muay Thai competitors on the coaching staff. Experienced Black Belt coaches on the BJJ mat. When we give you a correction, it's because we've felt it ourselves.

All levels, for real.

We have members who've been training 10 years and members who walked in last week. They train in the same facility, with the same coaches, at the same standards. We scale — we don't dumb it down.

The community is real.

Five stars and 144 Google reviews don't happen by accident. The members who train here stick around. That's the signal — people don't stay where they're just another number.

The facility is legit.

5,000 square feet. Six barbell racks, dumbbells, kettlebells, assault bikes, rowers, a functional trainer, heavy bags, ring space, and open mat. Not a converted strip mall. A real gym built for real training.

Peninsula-first since day one.

We're in Belmont. We serve Belmont, San Mateo, San Carlos, Redwood City, Foster City, and Burlingame. This is a local gym that knows its community — not a brand expanding into the market.

The Founder

JD
Jake DeIaco
Founding Owner & Program Director

Jake DeIaco is the founding owner and Program Director of Bay Area Boxing. His vision was to create a training facility that welcomes complete beginners while also challenging serious athletes, with a strong focus on boxing, Muay Thai kickboxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and strength training.

Sixteen years later, Bay Area Boxing has grown into one of the Peninsula's premier mixed martial arts training facilities. With programs for adults, kids, beginners, competitors, and families, Bay Area Boxing offers something for everyone in a supportive, high-energy training environment.

Jake brings decades of experience in martial arts, fitness, and gym management, including a Second-Degree Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu under Brian Fernandez, 16 years of BJJ training, 20 years of strength training experience, and 18 years of fitness management experience.

Head Coaches

The people who built this program. Each one a specialist. Each one the real deal.

JD
Jake DeIaco
Founding Owner & Program Director

Jake DeIaco founded Bay Area Boxing in 2010 with a vision to create a facility that welcomes complete beginners while challenging serious athletes. Jake holds a Second-Degree Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu under Brian Fernandez and brings 16 years of BJJ training, 20 years of strength training, and 18 years of fitness management experience.

BF
Brian Fernandez
Head Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Coach

Black Belt in Judo (1994, under Willy Cahill) and Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (2007). 20+ years of martial arts training and competition. Now retired from competition, Brian brings elite-level experience to the mat as Head BJJ Instructor. As a Black Belt, he can rank students up to his level.

VC
Victor “Vick” Chen
Muay Thai Coach

Victor's passion for martial arts started at age 8. His training began in 1993, and he went on to coach alongside competitors who fought in Strikeforce and the UFC. From 2009, Victor trained at the world-renowned Sasiprapa Gym in Thailand for 6+ years, and continues to train with top-level gyms in NYC, Amsterdam, and the UK. Coach Vick brings that depth to his Muay Thai fundamentals and pad work classes at Bay Area Boxing.

PM
Phillip Mendoza
Head Muay Thai Coach & Program Director

Phillip started martial arts training at a young age, picked up boxing in his teens, and began formally training and competing in Muay Thai in 2010. Over the years he developed a passion for teaching and a sharp eye for technique, with a focus on fundamentals — believing that building proper fundamentals is the most important part of improving in any discipline.

AB
Alexey Bulankov
Head Boxing Coach

USA Boxing-certified coach and competitive boxer. His classes focus on overall athleticism with an emphasis on technique — specifically the strategic and tactical aspects of boxing: footwork, ring generalship, and fight IQ. His background in Soviet-style boxing training methodology gives students an edge in becoming intelligent, well-rounded, and well-conditioned boxers.
