## Notes for Jake

1. **Video is non-negotiable.** Reels are how you get reach on Instagram in 2026. At minimum, 3 per week.
2. **Batch shoot when you can.** One hour on a Saturday = 2 weeks of content.
3. **Members are your best content.** Ask permission, then feature them constantly.
4. **Don't post and ghost.** Reply to every comment for the first hour after posting — Instagram rewards early engagement.
5. **The bio needs a link.** Use Linktree or direct to your booking page. Every CTA needs somewhere to land.
