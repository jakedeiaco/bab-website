# Bay Area Boxing — Instagram Content Strategy
_Built by Vin | March 2026_

---

## The Mission
Make Bay Area Boxing the most recognized combat sports gym in the Bay Area.
Turn Instagram from a community photo album into a member acquisition machine — without losing the authentic community feel that makes BAB special.

---

## The Content Mix (40/40/20)

### 40% — ACQUISITION CONTENT
_Designed to stop a stranger's scroll and make them want to train here._

- **The "You Should Be Here" Reel** — raw gym footage, pads getting cracked, rolls on the mat, the energy of a packed class
- **Fighter Spotlight** — a coach or competitive student, their story, why they fight
- **"First Day" POV** — what it actually feels like to walk in for the first time
- **Transformation posts** — physical and mental. Member stories.
- **The Hook Post** — controversial or bold statement. "Most people don't actually want to fight. That's fine. We train both."
- **Location/Local hooks** — "Belmont's best kept secret." "Peninsula fighters train here."

### 40% — COMMUNITY CONTENT
_Builds loyalty, gets shared by members, shows what you're really about._

- Belt promotions / milestone moments
- Competition results (wins AND losses — showing losses builds credibility)
- Event recaps
- Team photos
- "Member of the Month" features
- Behind-the-scenes (gym setup, early morning, after close)

### 20% — EDUCATION/VALUE CONTENT
_Positions BAB as the authority. Gets saved and shared._

- Technique breakdowns (short reels — jab mechanics, guard passing, clinch work)
- "Why we train ___" — the philosophy behind the discipline
- Training tips, recovery, mindset
- FAQ content — "How long until I can spar?" "What do I wear?"

## Posting Cadence

- **Reels:** 3x per week (Mon / Wed / Fri) — these drive reach
- **Static posts/carousels:** 2x per week (Tue / Thu) — community and education
- **Stories:** Daily — behind the scenes, polls, class reminders, quick clips

---

## Caption Formula

**Hook** (line 1 — must work without clicking "more")
→ **Body** (2-4 lines max — story, stat, or call to action)
→ **CTA** (one clear ask — DM us, link in bio, tag someone)
→ **Hashtags** (in first comment or at bottom — 10-15 max, mix of niche + local)

## Tone Rules

✅ DO:
- Write like a fighter talks. Direct. No fluff.
- Use "we" — this is a community
- Name the city. "Belmont." "Peninsula." "Bay Area."
- Show the work. The sweat. The technique.
- Let member voices in — quotes, tags, reshares

❌ DON'T:
- "Join our family!" (cringe)
- Generic motivational quotes
- Over-use exclamation points!!!
- Corporate-sounding anything
- Post without a caption hook
